# The Brambleguard

## Minotaur Base Stats

| HP | DEF | ACC | MOVE | AL |
|----|-----|-----|------|-----|
| 10 | 5 | 4+ | 6 | 2 |

## Team Rule: Mess with the Bull, Get the Horns

Whenever a Minotaur charges an enemy unit, deal d3 + 1 damage and knock back that enemy unit 1 hex. The minotaur can choose to follow the enemy (move the 1 hex to stay within reach) or it can stop where its charge ended. 

If you would end in REACH of two or more units, you can only select 1 to move back. You can still follow it.

If the enemy unit has terrain adjacent to them on a hex that isn't adjacent to the charging minotaur, you can choose not to move them and deal 2d3 damage instead.

**Leader Ability: Unstoppable Charge**
