## Team Rule: Treasure!

Whenever a Goblin performs a mission action or the "Look for Treasure" action, roll a d6: on a 4+, you find a treasure token. The amount of treasure tokens you're holding can give buffs:

| Tokens | Buff |
|--------|------|
| 2+ | When shooting or fighting, friendly goblins get to re-roll 1 attack dice |
| 4+ | All weapons get Crit 5+ |
| 6+ | Your Faction gets +3 DEF |
| 8+ | Add +1 Damage to all Power |
| 10+ | When shooting or fighting, friendly goblins get to re-roll any attack dice |

### Special Action: Look for Treasure

Until you move next, this unit has +1 DEF. This action cannot be performed within your drop zone.
