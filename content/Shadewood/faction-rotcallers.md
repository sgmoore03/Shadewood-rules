# The Rotcallers

## Myconid Base Stats

| HP | DEF | ACC | MOVE | AL |
|----|-----|-----|------|-----|
| 10 | 5 | 3+ | 6 | 2 |

## Team Rule: Death Bloom

Whenever a Myconid perishes, drop a death bloom token. The area of the death bloom is 2 x the turning point hexes within the token (on turn 2, within 4 hexes of the token, turn 3 is 6 hexes, etc.). 

Whenever a friendly Myconid is on a friendly death bloom, they get +1 damage to all power stats. 

Whenever an enemy is on a death bloom, they are -3 DEF and -1 move (to a minimum of 4).

**Leader Ability: Spreading Rot**

Once per battle, during this operative's activation, for free, you can command a friendly operative from your faction to complete 1 free Generic Action. If that action would make that friendly unit move, it can only move a maximum of 2 hexes.

**Special Action: Spot**
Selects a visible enemy target to be spotted. When a friendly unit within 3 hexes of this unit is shooting a spotted enemy, its shooting weapons get lethal 5+. An enemy is no longer spotted if it's currently not visible to this unit, or until one of the following happens:

- This unit uses this action again
