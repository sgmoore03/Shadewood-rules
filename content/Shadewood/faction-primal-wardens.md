## Centaur Base Stats

| HP | DEF | ACC | MOVE | AL |
|----|-----|-----|------|-----|
| 16 | 6 | 3+ | 7 | 3 |

## Team Rule: Slicing Gallop

Centaurs may move within and out of melee range on normal moves and charges. Any unit that they move in and out of melee range during one of these actions takes d3 wounds.

## Leaders

### Chieftain Swiftstrike

**Leader Ability: Relentless Pursuit**
Whenever a friendly Primal Warden uses Slicing Gallop and damages an enemy unit, that friendly operative may immediately move 1 additional hex for free.

**Special Action: Command**
Once per battle, during this operative's activation, for free, you can command a friendly operative from your faction to complete 1 free Generic Action. If that action would make that friendly unit move, it can only move a maximum of 2 hexes.
