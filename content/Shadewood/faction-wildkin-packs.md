# The Wildkin Packs

## Werewolf Base Stats

| HP | DEF | ACC | MOVE | AL |
|----|-----|-----|------|-----|
| 16 | 6 | 3+ | 6 | 3 |

## Team Rule: Scent of the Weak

A Werewolf gets +1 to all damage from attack dice for all attacks against enemy units that are not at full health. They also get +1 hex of charge range when charging an enemy unit not at full health.

## Leaders

### Alpha Bloodmoon

**Leader Ability: Blood Frenzy**
Whenever a friendly Wildkin operative damages an enemy unit, if that enemy unit is reduced below half health by that attack, all friendly Wildkin operatives get +1 hex of movement until the end of the turning point.

**Special Action: Command**
Once per battle, during this operative's activation, for free, you can command a friendly operative from your faction to complete 1 free Generic Action. If that action would make that friendly unit move, it can only move a maximum of 2 hexes.

**Weapon Profiles**

|---------|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Hunting Rifle | 4 | 3+ | 12 | 3/5 | - |
| Lupine Claws | 4 | 3+ | Melee | 4/5 | - |

### Packmaster Steelhowl

**Leader Ability: Pack Tactics**
All friendly Wildkin operatives get +1 to hit (improve accuracy by 1, e.g., 3+ becomes 2+) when shooting or fighting an enemy that is within 3 hexes of another friendly Wildkin operative.

**Special Action: Command**
Once per battle, during this operative's activation, for free, you can command a friendly operative from your faction to complete 1 free Generic Action. If that action would make that friendly unit move, it can only move a maximum of 2 hexes.

Heal a friendly unit within 2 hexes for d3 + 2. This can't be used on a friendly unit that has been revived this turn.

**Special Ability: Crippling Strike**
After this operative damages an enemy unit with a Fight action, that enemy is treated as not at full health for the rest of the turning point (even if healed back to full health). This allows other Wildkin operatives to benefit from Scent of the Weak against that target.
