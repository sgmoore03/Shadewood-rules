# The Bloodthorn Court

## Vampire Base Stats

| HP | DEF | ACC | MOVE | AL |
|----|-----|-----|------|-----|
| 16 | 6 | 4+ | 5 | 3 |

## Team Rule: Transfusion

Vampires can spend 2 health to upgrade an attack dice by 1 (this has no limit for number of uses per attack roll). For every natural 6 they roll, they regain 3 health. You can spend health and receive health in any order.

---

## Team Composition

**Leaders:** Choose 1
- Lord Crimson
- Lady Nightveil

**Operatives:** Choose 2
- Shadowblade Operative
- Bloodfire Suppressor
- Duskwatch Marksman

## Leaders

### Lord Crimson

**Leader Ability: Sanguine Mastery**
On natural 5's, the team heals for 1 health from their Transfusion ability.

**Special Action: Command**
Once per battle, during this operative's activation, for free, you can command a friendly operative from your faction to complete 1 free Generic Action. If that action would make that friendly unit move, it can only move a maximum of 2 hexes.

**Weapon Profiles**

| Profile | ATK | ACC | RNG | PWR | SR |
|---------|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Blood Rifle | 4 | 4+ | 12 | 3/4 | - |
| Sanguine Blade | 4 | 4+ | Melee | 4/5 | - |

**Special Ability: Skilled Fighter**
Has +3 DEF against melee attacks.

### Lady Nightveil

**Leader Ability: Warden's Presence**
All friendly Bloodthorn Court operatives get +1 DEF against shooting attacks. This bonus is doubled (+2 DEF total against shooting) for any vampire below half health.

**Special Action: Command**
Once per battle, during this operative's activation, for free, you can command a friendly operative from your faction to complete 1 free Generic Action. If that action would make that friendly unit move, it can only move a maximum of 2 hexes.

### Duskwatch Marksman
*Sniper / Spotter*

**Weapon Profiles**

| Profile | ATK | ACC | RNG | PWR | SR |
|---------|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Blood Hawk (Stationary) | 4 | 2+ | 16 | 3/6 | Stationary |
| Blood Hawk | 4 | 3+ | 12 | 3/5 | - |

**Special Ability: Roll**
After shooting with the stationary profile, this unit may move 1 hex for free.

**Stationary**
A unit cannot perform any movement actions before or after shooting this weapon, other than Roll.

**Special Action: Spot**
Selects a visible enemy target to be spotted. When a friendly unit within 3 hexes of this unit is shooting a spotted enemy, its shooting weapons get lethal 5+. An enemy is no longer spotted if it's currently not visible to this unit, or until one of the following happens:
- This unit is killed
- This unit uses this action again
- The next time this operative activates
